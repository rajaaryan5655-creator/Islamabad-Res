# Guest Guide

Everything you can do on **islamabadrestaurant.pk**.

---

## Ordering food

1. Open **Order Online**.
2. Choose **Delivery**, **Pickup** or **Dine in**.
3. For delivery, pick your area. You will see the charge, the minimum order and the
   expected time before you add anything.
4. Add dishes. Use **Notes for the kitchen** on any dish for things like *less spice*,
   *no coriander* or *extra raita* — the cooks see these on the ticket.
5. Go to **Checkout**, enter your details, apply a coupon if you have one, and pay.

You do not need an account to order. Signing in saves your address, earns points and
lets you reorder in one tap.

### Paying

Credit and debit card (via Stripe), PayPal, JazzCash, Easypaisa, or cash to the rider.
We never see or store your card number.

Prices on the menu exclude 16% government sales tax, which is shown as a separate line
at checkout so you can see exactly what you are paying.

### Tracking

After ordering you get a tracking link. It shows six stages:

**Pending → Confirmed → Preparing → Ready → Out for delivery → Delivered**

The page updates itself; you do not need to refresh. Signed-in customers can also find
it under **Orders** in their dashboard.

### Cancelling

Cancel free of charge while your order shows **Pending** or **Confirmed** — the button
is on the order page. Once the kitchen has started, please call **+92 306 4650507**.

## Booking a table

Open **Reservations**, choose your date, party size and seating preference, and the
grid shows live availability.

- A **plain box** is free — click it.
- A **dashed amber box** is fully booked; you can still choose it to join the waiting
  list, and we will call you if it opens up.
- A **gold dot** marks a peak slot (7–9pm).

Fill in your details and confirm. You get a **confirmation code** immediately — keep it.

**Changing or cancelling.** Free up to two hours before. Sign in and use **Reservations**
in your dashboard, or use your confirmation code.

We hold your table for 15 minutes past the booked time.

**Parties over 20** should use the **Events** page instead — the Faisal Room seats 20 and
our events manager will look after you.

### Special requests

Tell us in the booking form: high chair, wheelchair access, a quiet corner, a birthday
cake, or an allergy. The floor team sees these before you arrive.

## Allergies and dietary needs

Every dish lists its **allergens**, **calories** and **spice level**.

On the menu page, open **Filters** to:

- show vegetarian dishes only
- exclude specific allergens (gluten, dairy, nuts, egg, soy, mustard, sesame, fish)
- filter by spice level
- set a maximum price

Our kitchen handles nuts, dairy and gluten, so we cannot guarantee zero trace
cross-contact. **If you have a severe allergy, tell us when you order or book** and the
kitchen will take extra precautions.

All our meat is halal-certified and sourced fresh every morning. We hold a Grade A
certificate from the Islamabad Food Authority.

## The concierge

The red chat button, bottom right of every page. Ask it about:

- what to order, and what a dish contains
- prices
- delivery to your area and how long it takes
- booking a table
- allergies and vegetarian options
- opening hours, parking, directions
- loyalty points and current offers

It answers from our live menu, so prices and availability are always current.
